**2026-09-19** — Quantified the cost of undistorting to a pinhole model, ahead of choosing the pipeline's input camera format (`docs/pinhole_tradeoff.md`, `scripts/pinhole_tradeoff.py`, diagnostic only, no pipeline touched, CPU/embree, 3 sequences spanning segments: cecum/descending/sigmoid2). Deriving the "as much FOV as practical" pinhole took real iteration: a naive corner-angle-to-vignette match (f=362.66) turned out to overshoot the actual captured frame at the edge midpoints (up to 91px beyond the real image) because the omni model's stretch matrix makes it mildly anisotropic — replaced with an empirical full-perimeter validity search, giving f=541.29 (H-FOV 102.55° vs original ~122.4°, V-FOV 89.86° vs ~90.5° — vertical nearly free, horizontal costs ~20°). Corner resampling stretch factor: 3.38x (1 source pixel covers 3.38 destination pixels at the corner after undistortion) — a real resolution cost independent of the FOV loss. No recommendation made on which format to choose; numbers reported for that decision.

**2026-09-20** — Empirically validated EndoDAC's predicted pose convention against our frozen `.reshape(4,4).T` GT convention, before any trajectory work (`docs/pipelines/endodac.md` §7, `scratch/pipelines/endodac_pose_validation.py`, `endodac_pose_validation_perstep.py`).
