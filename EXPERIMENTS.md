**2026-09-17** — Dataset inventory (`docs/data_inventory.md`). Scanned `/data1_ycao/chua/datasets/C3VDv2` without bulk extraction; one representative sequence extracted to `scratch/`. Result: confirmed folder structure, modality formats/bit-depths, and cross-checked the summary-sheet index against archive names (192 index rows, all c1/c2/c0 archives accounted for). Open question at the time: none blocking; set up `docs/conventions.md` next to pin down the ambiguous parts (camera model, pose convention, coverage criterion).

**2026-09-17** — Camera/pose/coverage conventions verified empirically, not assumed (`docs/conventions.md`). Depth = uint16 linear 0-100mm camera-Z; pose.txt requires `.reshape(4,4).T`, not a plain reshape (empirically confirmed via oracle test, see below); camera model confirmed Scaramuzza omnidirectional with exact forward/inverse equations cited to `C3VDv3/render/Render.cu`; coverage GT criterion confirmed as pure ray-hit visibility, any frame, no distance/angle threshold, cited to source. Open question: none remaining after the oracle check below settled the pose-interpretation ambiguity.

**2026-09-17** — Oracle check: does depth+pose+camera-model reconstruct the released `coverage_mesh.obj`? (`docs/oracle_check.md`). Result: **PASS**. Median point-to-mesh distance 0.028mm (transposed pose interpretation; the "raw" interpretation is wrong by ~400mm, not ambiguous). Face-set IoU 0.87 at the tested sampling density, rising to 0.94 at 4x density — diagnosed as a sampling-density artifact of the validation script itself (false_observed stayed negligible throughout), not a pipeline bug.

**2026-09-18** — Coverage stats, all registered sequences (`docs/coverage_stats.md`). Streamed `coverage_mesh.obj` from every `registered_videos` zip (no extraction). Result: median 24.2% of surface area unobserved per sequence; cecum best-covered (single-opening blind pouch); boundary-distance breakdown shows most unobserved area is NOT an open-end artifact (median 80% farther than 5mm from any boundary edge). Bug found and fixed: one archive (`c1_cecum_t1_v3.zip`) wraps its contents in a subfolder unlike every other archive; loader now resolves the actual internal path instead of assuming root-level. Correction logged in the doc itself: sequence discovery was later tightened to *.zip-only (excluding 2 incidental extracted directories that have since disappeared from the dataset root), dropping 2 duplicate rows, 171->169.

**2026-09-18** — Open-end artifact check, 6 sequences (`docs/openend_artifact_check.md`). Rendered (pyrender/EGL) and geodesic-boundary-distance-analyzed the 5 largest unobserved components per sequence. Result: largest component always touches an open-end boundary but mostly consists of lumen wall well inside the tube (5-28% of its own area within 5mm of any edge) — a real coverage gap, not a rim artifact. ~50% of the next-4-largest components per sequence are genuinely isolated mid-wall (fold-occlusion) patches.

**2026-09-18** — Region-level analysis, all 169 sequences (`docs/regions.md`). 24,208 unobserved connected components extracted with per-region geometry (area, compactness, boundary distance, axis position) and mesh content-hashed for independence. Result: 169 sequences reduce to only **103 distinct meshes** and 113 distinct trajectories — v2/v3 share a mesh 83% of the time (not always), v1 shares v2's mesh only 31% of the time (usually an independently-scanned different mesh). `sigmoid`/`sigmoid1`/`sigmoid2` (colon-exclusive segments) are the strata most at risk of being treated as more independent than they are.

**2026-09-18** — Difficulty-subset classification (`docs/difficulty_subsets.md`). 169 sequences -> monolithic (56) / fragmented (15) / mixed (98) by largest-unobserved-component-fraction and component count. Fragmentation concentrated almost entirely in `ascending` (10/15). Added continuous `fragmentation_score_lt10mm` to `results/coverage_stats.csv` alongside the categorical label.

**2026-09-19** — GPU rasterizer validation before committing to a full run (`docs/gpu_validation.md`). Backend equivalence (Warp GPU vs embree CPU): 0.0057% pixel disagreement, all inspected cases shared-vertex ties — pass. GT `raw==0` cross-check initially looked like a red flag (GT has ~7% zero pixels, we have ~0%) but resolved by source citation: it's a vignette mask applied only to saved framebuffer images (`RenderingModule.cpp:273-278`), never to the coverage buffer — our near-zero miss rate is correct, not a bug. `MAX_DEPTH` cited exactly: gates only the depth-image encoding (`Render.cu:330`), not the coverage/observed criterion (`Render.cu:316-320`, primary ray tmax=1e4 at line 223). Accuracy vs released mesh at stride 1: IoU 0.9988, target (>=0.99) met.

**2026-09-19** — Full 169-sequence GPU visibility rasterization (`docs/visibility_rasterization.md`). Result: **19.3 minutes** total, median IoU 0.9988 (mean 0.9976, min 0.9731). 7 sequences flagged IoU<0.99 for later inspection (not diagnosed): `c2_transverse2_t3_v1, c1_sigmoid1_t2_v2, c1_ascending_t4_v3, c1_ascending_t4_v2, c1_sigmoid1_t2_v3, c2_rectum_t4_v1, c2_rectum_t3_v1` — all 7 show `false_unobserved` 4-25x `false_observed`, unlike the balanced noise in the validated baseline sequence; worth investigating before trusting these 7 downstream. Bug found and fixed mid-run: `stream_poses_from_zip()` (new code) didn't inherit the zip-subfolder-resolution fix `stream_coverage_mesh_from_zip()` already had, so it crashed loudly (not silently) on the one known-exceptional archive; fixed by sharing the resolver, verified on 17 sequences spanning the fix point before relaunching. Also found and fixed a ~40x CPU-side bottleneck in the ray-transform code (4.5fps -> 41-64fps) before the real run, without which the "GPU path" would have taken over 2 hours despite the GPU kernel itself being fast. **Open question**: diagnose the 7 flagged sequences' false_unobserved-dominant pattern.

**2026-09-19** — Diagnosed the 9 worst/flagged sequences' false_unobserved faces (`docs/visibility_outliers.md`), reusing saved masks, no GPU rerun. Result: the user's requested 2-way (a)/(b) grouping is not geometrically homogeneous — pattern (a) splits into two opposite sub-signatures. **Group B (sigmoid1, 3 seqs)**: sub-pixel/grazing-angle hypothesis holds cleanly — footprint 150-200x smaller than control, 70-92% under 1px², single dominant frame per sequence with no pose anomaly, zero mesh degeneracy found (duplicate/near-zero-area triangles ruled out). **Group A1 (ascending, 3 seqs)**: hypothesis does NOT hold — footprint is *larger* than control, not smaller; visual inspection shows a dense speckle running exactly along the observed/unobserved boundary contour (near-1:1 component:face ratio, 99%+ rim-adjacent), suggesting a boundary-alignment discrepancy instead, marked as an unverified interpretation. **Group A2 (transverse2/rectum, 3 seqs)**: mixed — 2 of 3 partially fit the grazing-angle story, `c2_rectum_t4_v1` fits none of the tested hypotheses and is left as UNKNOWN. Nothing was fixed (diagnosis only, per instructions); frozen camera/pose conventions were not touched. **Open questions**: (1) does supersampling frame 122/103 recover group B's faces — would confirm the sub-pixel mechanism; (2) a screen-space boundary-proximity measurement for group A1, not yet computed; (3) `c2_rectum_t4_v1` remains unexplained.

**2026-09-19** — Confirmation experiment: 2x2 sub-pixel supersampling at the specific frames identified above (`docs/visibility_outliers.md` closing section, `scripts/supersample_confirmation.py`, CPU/embree, diagnostic only, pipeline unchanged). Result: matches the pre-registered "two distinct causes" branch, but with a magnitude nobody had anticipated — group A1 (ascending) shows ~0% recovery (indistinguishable from GPU-vs-CPU backend tie-breaking noise, ~0.006%, already known from `docs/gpu_validation.md`), while group B (sigmoid1) shows 2.7-3.8% net recovery, 10-100x A1's rate but far short of "most." Caught and corrected a confound before trusting the raw numbers: a 1x sanity cast at these same frames should recover exactly 0 false_unobserved faces by definition and didn't (backend noise), so all reported recovery is net of that baseline and measured against the correct denominator (only faces whose recorded best-frame is the one tested). **Disposition**: group A1 logged as UNKNOWN, boundary-alignment hypothesis still leading but unverified; group B's sub-pixel explanation supported but not fully confirmed (these faces are directionally sensitive to sampling density but 2x2 isn't enough to recover most of them). No pipeline or metric change made. Diagnosis of the 9 flagged sequences is now closed per instructions.

**2026-09-19** — Closed out visibility validation: `docs/visibility_limitations.md` written for paper reuse.
